Admission Process & Eligibility

Follow these simple steps to start your MBBS journey:

Eligibility Criteria

  • ✔ Age: 17+ years
  • ✔ Qualification: 10+2 with at least 50% in PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • ✔ NEET Requirement: Required for Indian students planning to practice in India

Application Process

  1. Fill the Application Form - Submit basic details & required documents.
  2. Receive Admission Letter - Get confirmation from the university.
  3. Visa Processing - Assistance for smooth visa approval.
  4. Flight & Arrival - Travel arrangements & hostel setup.
  5. Start Your MBBS Journey - Begin your medical education abroad!
